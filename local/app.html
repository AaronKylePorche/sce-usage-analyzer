Rows with no usage recorded are skipped, so a range that includes days SCE has not posted yet will show fewer days than the billing period. Running through serve.py, daily highs are fetched live from Open-Meteo for your exact ZIP and your file’s own date range, then cached on disk. Opened without the server, the page falls back to built-in highs for 13 Southern California climate zones from Sep 1 2024 on. Your spreadsheet is read in the browser either way and never leaves this computer.

Energy charges only. This leaves out the daily basic charge, any minimum-charge adjustment, taxes and local surcharges, so your actual bill will land somewhat higher. On TOU-D-PRIME the weekday 4–9pm window is the expensive purchase: running the dishwasher or starting an EV charge after 9pm moves those kilowatt-hours to the Off-Peak price without using any less power. Temperature and cost sit on two different scales here, so read the line for its shape against the columns, not for where it crosses them — the scatter above is the honest test of how tightly the two actually move together.
